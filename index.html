whoami

I'm a student who has spent the last five years living inside Linux — not as a hobby I dabble in, but as my daily environment. significant_project
Linux Distribution

Vajra Linux

A custom fork of MX Linux that I designed and built myself. It adds a simplified desktop layout switcher so users can change their interface with ease, and full support for Indian regional languages — the locale, fonts, and input methods needed to make Linux genuinely usable for people who aren't comfortable in English.

Building it meant working hands-on with system internals, package customization, and localization frameworks — the kind of work that only makes sense once you actually understand the machine underneath. It's also where my two interests meet: making powerful technology more accessible to the people who are usually left out of it.
